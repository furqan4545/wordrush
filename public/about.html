Wordrush turns a short phrase into a vertical MP4. It opens with your words typed into a filmed browser address bar, then cuts through 50 edited website scenes in five seconds.

How do I make a video?

Enter up to eight words, optionally starting with “How to.” Adjust motion blur if you want, then select Create. Watch the preview and use the download icon to save your MP4.

What happens if I start with “How to”?

The opening types your complete query. A leading “How to” is left out of the phrase used inside the website scenes. For example, “how to become great” appears in the search intro, and “become great” appears in the cuts.

How are the website scenes made?

Wordrush edits your phrase into paragraphs in reusable website backgrounds. These excerpts are created for the video. The montage contains 50 cuts at 0.1 seconds each.

What kind of video can I download?

You get a vertical 9:16 MP4 at 1080 × 1920, with typing, Enter and transition sounds. The total length includes the typing intro plus the five-second montage.

Where does video rendering happen?

Your device renders the video in a browser worker. Completed videos are saved in local browser storage on that device. Each browser has its own video library, with room for up to eight saved videos. Use the trash icon beside a title to delete a video and free space.
